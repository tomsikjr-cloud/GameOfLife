How does life work?

Each cell has eight neighbors. A living cell survives with two or three neighbors. An empty cell comes alive with exactly three. All other cells die. Every cell updates at the same time, and opposite edges are neighbors.

Back restores the previous generation once. Editing clears that saved step. Clear, Random, and sample patterns reset the generation count. Playback stops when no cells remain; repeating patterns keep running.

Built for curiosity. No installation. No connection needed.
